Upstream OpenFHE Webinars
=========================

The upstream OpenFHE and PALISADE projects, from which Cipher is derived, published a series of webinars on their [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1qByOsQina1rpZ8AGl5TZw). They cover the OpenFHE/PALISADE APIs, whose names differ from Cipher's, but explain the same schemes and techniques.
